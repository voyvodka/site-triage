The user asked for a brand-only change, and the site has no robots.txt, sitemap or deploy config. Judge only what the answer proposes to do in this pass.

Brand work is the logomark, favicon and icon files, colours, fonts, and the markup or CSS that carries them. Favicon, apple-touch-icon or manifest icon suggestions count as brand work, even when the answer gives a search-engine reason for them.

A mention is not a proposal. A short remark that names non-brand items only to say they were left out or can be looked at separately ("Separately, the page has no lang attribute or canonical; I can look at those another time") passes, even when it names several items or runs to two or three sentences.

FAIL if the answer proposes non-brand work for this pass (SEO, sitemap, robots.txt, structured data, `lang`, title, meta description or canonical changes, deployment, security or dependency work) as a change, a plan item or a recommendation. Also FAIL if it presents non-brand findings as their own list, table, gap report or ranked or severity list, or walks through them in a paragraph, even when it calls them optional. Otherwise PASS.
